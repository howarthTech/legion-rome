A past celebration of the American Legion's founding. Members of Post 5, Unit 5,
and Squadron 5 gathered at Landmark Family Restaurant for dinner, fellowship,
and recognition of the Legion's century of service.

The American Legion was chartered by Congress on **September 16, 1919**, but
many posts hold annual birthday observances in March, marking the original
Paris Caucus of March 15–17, 1919, where the organization was founded.
