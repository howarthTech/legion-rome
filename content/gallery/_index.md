Browse photos from Post 5 meetings, ceremonies, community service events, and
gatherings at The Farm. Click any album below to view its photos.

Albums are managed in the post's CRM (the Photo gallery screen) and build into
this page automatically. The content adapter next to this file pulls each album
and its photos from the CRM's public gallery API at build time. The Farm album
below is pre-seeded in this repo and stays alongside any CRM albums.
