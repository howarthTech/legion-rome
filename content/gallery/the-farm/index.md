The Farm is our regular meeting location and the venue for many of our
community events. The grounds include a covered picnic pavilion, a gazebo
overlooking the pond, and open lawn for larger gatherings.

The Farm is also available to rent for private events — see our
[Hall Rental](/rental/) page for details.
