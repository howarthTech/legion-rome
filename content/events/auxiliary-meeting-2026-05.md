The American Legion Auxiliary Unit 5 holds its monthly meeting at Brookdale
Senior Living, the same evening as the Post 5 meeting but at a separate
location.

Prospective Auxiliary members are welcome to attend and learn about eligibility,
the organization's national mission, and Unit 5's local programs.
