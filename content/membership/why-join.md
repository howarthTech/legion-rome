## Your Buddy Still Needs You

The friendships forged in service don't end at discharge. American Legion Post 5
exists so that veterans of every era — and from every branch — can stay
connected, support one another, and continue to serve.

When you join Post 5, you join:

- **Veterans you'll recognize.** Members of Post 5 served in every conflict and
  every branch from World War II to the Global War on Terror. The shared
  experience is real, and the community at Post 5 reflects it.
- **A century of community impact.** Since 1925, Post 5 has supported veterans,
  their families, and the broader Rome community. Your membership keeps that
  going.
- **National voice.** With over 1.8 million members nationally, The American
  Legion advocates for veterans' benefits, healthcare, and rights at every
  level of government.

## What We Do — Locally

Membership at Post 5 isn't a passive thing. Here's what members are involved in
throughout the year:

- **Funeral honor guards** for veterans of every branch, regardless of Legion
  membership — a service we provide free to the families of those who served.
- **Memorial Day and Veterans Day observances** at local cemeteries and
  community gatherings.
- **Scholarships** for Rome and Floyd County students.
- **Sponsorship of Boys State** delegates from local high schools.
- **The Oratorical Contest** — supporting high school students in their study
  of the U.S. Constitution.
- **Parades, memorial dedications, and community events** throughout Rome and
  Floyd County.

## What You Get

Beyond the camaraderie and the chance to give back, members of The American
Legion qualify for **member-only discounts** on travel, healthcare services,
and many consumer products — see [legion.org/benefits](https://www.legion.org/benefits)
for the national benefits catalog.
