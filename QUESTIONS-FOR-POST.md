need:

### Pricing
- Rate for **members** vs **non-members**
- **Half-day** vs **full-day** rates
- Weekend / holiday surcharge?
- Hourly availability?

### Capacity & amenities
- Maximum number of guests (indoor / outdoor / total)
- Number of tables and chairs included
- Kitchen access — what's provided, what isn't

- Parking — how many vehicles fit

### Policies
- Alcohol policy — BYO permitted? Licensed bar?
- Smoking policy
- Pets / children
- Cleanup requirements
- Insurance requirements for renters

### Booking process
- Deposit amount
- Cancellation terms
- Who is the rental contact — Commander? Adjutant? Someone else?
- Lead time required (X days in advance minimum?)

### Photos
We have one good photo of the grounds (with the pavilion, gazebo, and pond).
**More photos would help bookings** — interior shots of the meeting hall, the
kitchen, the parking area, examples of past events held there.

## For Anyone

### Legion Riders chapter
Does Post 5 have an active **Legion Riders** chapter? If not, we should
remove the stub page and the link from the nav. If yes, we need:
- Director / officers
- Eligibility / how to join
- Regular ride schedule
- Notable rides or events the chapter participates in

### Photo gallery
The new site has a working gallery — it shows one sample album ("The Farm")
using the two scenery photos we already have. **We need more albums to make
this useful**, especially for events the post has held in the last year or two.

Good candidates if photos exist:
- Memorial Day 2025 / 2026
- Veterans Day 2025
- Fourth of July party (any year)
- "We Care Vet Fair 2025" (referenced on the old site)
- Birthday Bash dinner
- Honor guard appearances
- Scholarship presentations
- The American Legion Family gatherings

For each album we need:
- A short title (e.g., "Memorial Day 2025")
- The date of the event
- 5–20 photos (more is fine, fewer is fine)
- A one-line description of what the album shows
- **Captions for each photo** are nice but not required — without them photos
  just display in a grid

Photos can be high-res straight from a phone or camera; the site resizes them
automatically at build time. Easiest delivery: a Google Drive folder, Dropbox
link, or a thumb drive.

Rick Hunt (Adjutant) is currently listed with phone only — does he have an
