The Sons of the American Legion (SAL) was created in 1932 to give the male
descendants of American Legion-eligible veterans a way to honor their service
and continue the work of the Legion. **Squadron 5** is the SAL chapter
attached to Post 5.

## When and Where We Meet

Squadron 5 meets at **The Farm**, 493 Jones Bend Rd NE, Rome, GA — separately
from the Post 5 monthly meeting. See the [Events](/events/) page for the next
scheduled meeting.

## Membership Eligibility

You may join the Sons of the American Legion if you are a **male descendant**,
adopted son, or stepson of:

- A current member of The American Legion, or
- A veteran whose service would have made them eligible for Legion membership,
  even if they died before joining

Eligible relationships include sons, grandsons, great-grandsons, and so on.
There is no age limit and no upper bound on degree of descent.
