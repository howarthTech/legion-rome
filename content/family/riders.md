The American Legion Riders is the motorcycle-riding extension of the Legion
family. Founded in 1993, ALR members participate in fundraising rides for
scholarships and veteran charities, escort military funerals as part of the
Patriot Guard, and ride in community parades and Memorial Day observances.

To join Legion Riders, you must first be a member of The American Legion, the
Auxiliary, or Sons of the American Legion.

For information about a possible Post 5 Riders chapter, [contact the post](/contact/).
