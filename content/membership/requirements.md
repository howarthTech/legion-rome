## Eligibility

To join The American Legion at Post 5, you must have served at least **one day
of active military duty** during one of the following qualifying periods, **and**
have been **honorably discharged** or still be serving honorably:

- **April 6, 1917 – November 11, 1918** (World War I)
- **December 7, 1941 to present** (any service since the start of World War II)

The "Legion Act" of 2019 effectively means anyone who served honorably after
Pearl Harbor is eligible — no more "war era" gaps. If you served honorably
since December 7, 1941, you qualify.

## What to Bring

To complete your membership:

1. **Membership application** — two printed copies. Use our online
   [application form](/membership/apply/), or stop by the Post.
2. **Proof of service** — a copy of your **DD214** (please black out your
   Social Security number for your own protection).
3. **Annual dues** — **$55** per year, paid by check or money order to
   "American Legion Post 5."

Upon approval, you'll receive your official American Legion membership card.

## Transferring Your Membership

Already a member at another post and want to move your membership to Post 5?
You'll need:

- A completed [application](/membership/apply/) (marked **Transfer**)
- A copy of your DD214
- Your current American Legion membership card
- Annual dues for the current year

Submit through our [online form](/membership/apply/) or contact the Adjutant,
**Rick Hunt**, at (912) 312-0258.

## Questions
