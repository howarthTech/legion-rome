The Four Pillars

Every American Legion post — including Post 5 — is built on four enduring pillars that guide our work in Rome and across the country.

Veterans Affairs & Rehabilitation

Advocating for and serving veterans, their families, and survivors.

National Security

Supporting a strong national defense and our active-duty service members.

Americanism

Promoting patriotism, citizenship, and respect for our flag and constitution.

Children & Youth

Supporting programs like Boys State, Oratorical Contests, and scholarships.
