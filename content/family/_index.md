The American Legion Family at Post 5 includes three affiliated organizations,
each with its own membership, officers, and programs. Together we serve
veterans, families, and the Rome community.

- **[Auxiliary (Unit 5)](/family/auxiliary/)** — eligible female relatives of veterans
- **[Sons of the American Legion (Squadron 5)](/family/sons/)** — eligible male descendants of veterans
- **[Legion Riders](/family/riders/)** — motorcycle-riding members of the Legion family
