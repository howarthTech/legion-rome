The American Legion Auxiliary is one of the world's oldest patriotic women's
organizations. Founded in 1919, today's Auxiliary has nearly **1 million
members** in 9,500+ communities. **Unit 5** is the chapter at Post 5 in Rome.

## When and Where We Meet

> **2nd Monday of each month · 6:00 PM**
> Brookdale Senior Living
> 180 Woodrow Wilson Way, Rome, GA 30165

The Auxiliary meets the same evening as Post 5, at a separate location nearby.

## Membership Eligibility

You may be eligible to join the Auxiliary if you are a female relative of a
veteran who served honorably during one of the qualifying war eras — including:

- A current member of The American Legion, or
- A veteran whose service would have made them eligible (even if deceased before
  ever joining)

Eligible relatives include grandmothers, mothers, sisters, wives, daughters,
granddaughters, and great-granddaughters of qualifying veterans.
