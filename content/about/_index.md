Shanklin Attaway American Legion Post 5 was chartered in **1925** and has served
veterans and the Rome, Georgia community for over a century. We are part of
[The American Legion](https://www.legion.org/), the nation's largest wartime
veterans service organization.

## Where We Meet

## The American Legion Family

Our post is the parent organization for two affiliated groups, both of which
have their own membership, officers, and activities:

- **[Auxiliary (Unit 5)](/family/auxiliary/)** — for eligible female relatives of veterans
- **[Sons of the American Legion (Squadron 5)](/family/sons/)** — for eligible male descendants of veterans

Together we are the American Legion Family. Each group supports the others'
activities and runs its own programs throughout the year.
