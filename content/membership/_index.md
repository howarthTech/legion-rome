The American Legion is the largest veterans service organization in the United
States, with **over 1.8 million members** across nearly 13,000 posts worldwide.
We were founded in 1919, and Post 5 has served Rome since 1925.

If you served at least **one day of active military duty** during a qualifying
period and received an **honorable discharge** (or are still serving honorably),
you're eligible to join.

## Three Ways to Belong

The American Legion Family at Post 5 includes three organizations. Pick the one
that fits — and you can support the others' activities regardless.

- **[The American Legion (Post 5)](/membership/requirements/)** — for veterans who served honorably
- **[Auxiliary (Unit 5)](/family/auxiliary/)** — for eligible female relatives of veterans
- **[Sons of the American Legion (Squadron 5)](/family/sons/)** — for eligible male descendants of veterans

## Already a Member?

If you're already a member nationally and want to **transfer** your membership
to Post 5, you'll need your current membership card, a copy of your DD214, and
the annual dues payment. Submit them with our [application](/membership/apply/)
form or contact our [Adjutant, Rick Hunt](/contact/), at {{< phone "(912) 312-0258" "Rick Hunt" >}}.
